Our Story

Born in Accra, built for the world

Kojosropa started with a simple frustration: finding truly unique clothing shouldn't be this hard. Founded in Ghana, we saw an abundance of incredible fashion — vintage, handmade, rare — trapped in local markets with no global stage.

We built the bridge. A marketplace where every item is hand-sourced, carefully authenticated, and listed once. No mass production, no fast fashion cycles. Just garments with history, character, and a single owner waiting to find them.

Today, Kojosropa connects conscious buyers with one-of-a-kind pieces from across West Africa and beyond. We believe fashion should be personal, intentional, and rare.

What We Stand For

The principles behind every listing

01

Quality over quantity

We'd rather list ten extraordinary pieces than a thousand mediocre ones. Every item earns its place through craft and condition.

02

No restocks, ever

Once a piece sells, it's gone for good. This isn't scarcity marketing — it's the nature of genuine one-of-a-kind fashion.

03

Sustainable fashion

By extending the life of existing garments, we reduce waste and celebrate the longevity that quality clothing deserves.
